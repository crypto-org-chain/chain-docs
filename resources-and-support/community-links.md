# Community Links

Cronos is powered by a global community of builders, validators, and users. The best way to stay informed and get involved is through the official channels:

* [Developer community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/5JTk2ppsY3): chat with the team and community, get support
* [Telegram Announcement Channel](https://t.me/Cronos_Announcements): key updates delivered straight to your feed
* [Proposal discussion](https://github.com/crypto-org-chain/chain-main/discussions): propose ideas and gather community feedback before going on-chain
* [Substack Blog Post](https://blog.cronos.com): long-form announcements and deep dives
* [X](https://x.com/CronosNetwork): news, ecosystem highlights, and real-time announcements

Pick the channels that fit you and jump in — the community is open to everyone.
